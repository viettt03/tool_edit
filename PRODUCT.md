# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Vietnamese creators who collect short-form footage for narrated story videos. They work locally on a Mac, want a fast way to turn a Douyin link into a usable clip, and prefer a focused tool over a large editing suite.

## Product Purpose

Cliproom is the local ingest surface for the Vietnamese video editor. It makes the first step obvious: paste a Douyin video, user, or search source, download clips into the editor library, and see what is ready for the next rendering step.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, editorial. The interface should feel like a small creator tool with clear decisions, quiet confidence, and useful feedback.

## Anti-references

Avoid overloaded admin dashboards, noisy neon creator software, unexplained technical jargon, and interfaces that hide download errors behind a spinner.

## Design Principles

- Make the next action obvious.
- Show the state of every download.
- Keep the local workflow transparent.
- Prefer useful density over decorative complexity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use clear labels, visible keyboard focus, sufficient contrast, semantic controls, and text equivalents for status. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
